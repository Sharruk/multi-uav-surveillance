# 📝 Update Rules for Team Members

To keep everyone on the team aligned and prevent the repository state from becoming a mystery, we follow a strict documentation rule.

---

## 🚨 The Golden Update Rule

Every time you commit a major update, new feature, or fix, you **MUST** update these three files in the root folder:

### 1. `CURRENT_STATUS.md`
*   **What goes here:** The current state of the application.
*   **Purpose:** Summarizes what works right now, what is broken/blocked, and known issues.
*   **Example:** "Updated Ray RLlib environment config. Verified that code builds under Python 3.10."

### 2. `CHANGELOG.md`
*   **What goes here:** A simple history of changes made.
*   **Format:** Date | Branch/Person | What Changed | Why | Issues Fixed | Next Step.
*   **Purpose:** Tracks progress over time so we can pinpoint when and why a bug was introduced.

### 3. `TASKS.md`
*   **What goes here:** The TODO list of the project.
*   **Purpose:** Marks completed tasks, keeps pending tasks organized, and lists new research ideas.

---

> Keep your updates short, clear, and practical. It takes less than 2 minutes and saves hours of team debug time!
